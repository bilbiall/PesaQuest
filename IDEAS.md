# PesaQuest — Game Extension Ideas

Brainstormed 2026-07-02. Build on these after current bugs are fixed.

---

## The Core Problem

Right now the game has **destinations but not activities**. Players visit a district, do one thing (buy, enroll, apply), then they're done. There's nothing pulling them to stay or return. A 30-minute session needs **loops** — reasons to keep moving around the map.

---

## Building Blocks Already in Place

- A map players physically navigate
- Jobs that pay salary over time
- Savings with deposits
- Assets and marketplace
- Quests with rewards
- Levels and XP
- A notification/event system
- A "while you were away" mechanic

---

## Ideas by Category

### 1. The Daily Financial Newspaper
Every session, a "Pesa City Herald" appears with 3–5 real events:
- "Tech stocks are up 18% this week — visit Equity Square"
- "Flash sale at Marketplace — gadgets 25% off for 2 hours"
- "Bank is offering double interest deposits today only"
- "Job fair at Opportunity Hub — new positions added"

Players who act on these tips earn more. Players who ignore them miss out. Creates a reason to log in daily and visit multiple districts in one session.

---

### 2. District Activities (Each District Has a "Thing to Do")

Give each district a repeatable daily activity beyond buying/applying:

| District | Daily Activity |
|----------|----------------|
| Bank | Deposit to savings scheme + interest rate mini-game |
| Equity Square | Check portfolio performance, rebalance holdings |
| Community | Chama contribution round + group savings leaderboard |
| Quest Board | Financial trivia — 5 questions, earn XP + KES |
| Fun World | Spin wheel — daily random reward (KES, XP, asset, quest boost) |
| Workplace | Job performance check — 3 questions → pass = raise |
| Marketplace | Daily deal — one discounted item, limited stock |
| Estates | Collect rent from property you own |
| Car Yard | Earn delivery income if you own a vehicle |
| Opportunity Hub | New freelance gig posted — time-limited, claim first |

A player who visits all 10 districts and does each activity has a full 30-minute session naturally.

---

### 3. City Contracts (Time-Limited Gigs)
Short tasks that appear on the map, stay for 30–60 minutes (real time), then expire:
- "Mombasa Traders need a web report — requires Web Dev course — reward: KES 800"
- "Community needs someone to run the stall — reward: KES 400 + 50 XP"
- "Bank needs a courier between Equity Square and Bank — walk your pin there — reward: KES 250"

The "walk your pin there" mechanic uses map navigation as gameplay itself. Creates urgency and makes the map feel alive.

---

### 4. Financial Events That Hit the Whole City
Economy-wide events that last a few hours or a day:
- "Inflation hits Pesa City — all prices up 15%, act fast"
- "Power cuts in Estates — property income reduced this week"
- "Youth employment drive — all entry jobs pay 20% more this week"
- "Market crash — investments drop, great time to buy"

Players in-app when these fire get notified and can respond. Creates FOMO and urgency.

---

### 5. The Career Ladder Inside Jobs
Jobs currently have one level. Add 3 levels of progression:
- Junior → Mid → Senior (requires a course + passing a performance check)
- Each level increases salary significantly
- Performance tested by 3 financial questions
- Promotion notification draws player back to Workplace

Extends Opportunity Hub value considerably — not just getting a job, but building a career.

---

### 6. Passive Income Management
Assets earn money passively but also need attention:
- Property earns rent each cycle, but occasionally a maintenance alert fires (costs KES to fix or tenant leaves)
- Car earns delivery income but needs a weekly fuel top-up
- Investments pay dividends that must be "claimed" by visiting the district

Creates reasons to return. Players who ignore assets start losing passive income.

---

### 7. Financial Trivia at Quest Board
A standalone trivia table at the Quest Board:
- 5 questions per session, resets daily
- Kenyan financial knowledge: M-Pesa fees, savings interest, tax brackets, budgeting rules
- Correct answer: XP + KES bonus
- Optional social power-up: share to get 1 free hint
- Pure activity, no setup, deeply educational, very replayable

---

### 8. The Chama Challenge
Community district runs a weekly group competition:
- All chama members compete to deposit the most in 7 days
- Top 3 depositors earn a bonus multiplier on their savings
- Progress board shows current ranking
- Social engagement even in a single-player context

---

### 9. City Reputation Score
A reputation number (separate from XP/level) that unlocks perks:

Built by: paying loans on time, hitting savings targets, completing quests, positive financial behaviour
Lost by: defaulting loans, failing performance reviews, ignoring property maintenance

Perks at high reputation:
- Better loan rates from the bank
- Exclusive job tiers at Opportunity Hub
- VIP deals at Marketplace
- Access to premium investment deals at Equity Square

Adds a second progression axis. Players optimise for both level and reputation.

---

### 10. Net Worth Milestone Unlock Gates
Visible thresholds on the map that unlock when net worth grows:
- KES 5,000 → Equity Square investments unlock
- KES 15,000 → Property Quarter opens
- KES 30,000 → Business ownership option
- KES 75,000 → Senior-level jobs available
- KES 150,000 → Stock trading tier

The map literally changes as you grow wealthier. Districts start glowing and opening up. This is the single biggest driver of "just 10 more minutes."

---

## What a 30-Minute Session Looks Like With All This

1. Log in → collect daily bonus + WYWA catches up salary/rent (2 min)
2. Read Pesa City Herald — spot a flash sale and a job fair (1 min)
3. Visit Marketplace → grab the discounted asset before it sells out (2 min)
4. Opportunity Hub → job fair is live, apply for a better position (3 min)
5. Quest Board trivia — 5 questions, earn XP (3 min)
6. New city contract appeared — walk pin to Equity Square to claim it (3 min)
7. Bank → deposit savings + double interest event is live (2 min)
8. Chama contribution — check weekly ranking (2 min)
9. Job performance review notification → answer 3 questions → get a raise (3 min)
10. Workplace → promotion unlocked, new salary kicks in (2 min)
11. Net worth crossed KES 5,000 — Equity Square investing unlocked (1 min)
12. Collect rent from property, deal with quick maintenance alert (3 min)
13. Quest completed automatically during session (notification) (1 min)
14. Check leaderboard — climbed 3 positions (1 min)

**29 minutes of natural, varied activity — no padding.**

## Build Priority

### High impact, lower effort (build first)
- District daily activities (spin wheel, trivia, daily deal)
- City contracts (timed gigs on the map)
- Financial newspaper / daily events

### High impact, medium effort
- Career ladder progression inside jobs
- Net worth milestone unlock gates
- Passive income + management alerts

### High impact, bigger build
- City reputation score
- Economy-wide events
- Chama competitive challenges
