# 🧰 The Smart Money Tools Guide — Where the Game Ends and Your Life Begins

*A player's guide to the Money Toolkit: 6 in-game calculators, plus real-life tools that
work on your actual money, on the actual calendar — a Premium feature.*

---

## The one-sentence version

Pesa City teaches you the *thinking*. The Money Toolkit is where that thinking becomes
*muscle memory* — first with practice numbers in the game, then, if you want, with your
own real money in real life. **Nothing in the "Real-Life" half of this toolkit ever
touches your Pesa City balance, credit score, or progress.** It's a separate room in the
same house.

---

## 1. Where to find it

Scroll down on your **Dashboard** to the **🧰 Smart Money Tools** section. This entire
toolkit — all 6 calculators plus the real-life tools — is a **Premium feature**. If
you're on a free account you'll see a locked preview with a link to subscribe; everyone
else gets the full toolkit, always available, no separate unlock needed once you're
subscribed.

## 2. The 6 In-Game Calculators

These use practice numbers you type in — nothing here is saved to a real calendar, and
nothing here affects your Pesa City progress either. They exist purely so you can *play*
with the maths of real financial decisions before making them for real.

| Tool | What it does | The "aha" moment it's built for |
|---|---|---|
| 💼 **Bajeti Smart** | Splits any income using Kenya's 50/30/20 rule with interactive sliders | "Oh — my rent is eating into my *wants* budget, not just my needs." |
| 🎯 **Lengo Saver** | Multiple savings goals, each with its own progress ring | "I can save toward my phone AND my emergency fund at the same time." |
| 📊 **Matumizi Track** | Logs daily spending by category | "I had NO idea I spent that much on airtime this week." |
| 📈 **Ukuaji Grow** | Compares compound growth across M-Pesa, Bank FD, SACCO, T-Bills and NSE stocks | "Ksh 1,000/month really does become that much more in a SACCO than under my mattress." |
| 🏦 **Mkopo Planner** | True cost of any loan before you borrow — monthly payment, total interest | "This loan actually costs me an EXTRA how much?!" |
| 💹 **Faida Compounder** | Initial + monthly contributions, visualised growing over years | Einstein's "8th wonder of the world" — but with your own numbers. |

Every one of these opens with a tap and closes the same way — no page changes, no lost
place. Play with them as often as you like.

## 3. Real-Life Tools — the part that leaves the game

This is the newest, most important part of the toolkit, and it works differently on
purpose: **it runs on real calendar dates, not game days**, because your real rent isn't
due "in 7 game days" — it's due on the 5th of next month. You'll see a clear
**"Real dates, not game days"** badge on this section so you always know which world
you're in.

### 🔔 Real Bill Reminders

Add your **actual** real-life bills — rent, electricity, school fees, a loan repayment,
a subscription — and PesaQuest will send you a **real push notification** before each
one is due, on your phone, whether the app is open or not.

**How to set one up:**
1. Open the Money Toolkit → **Real Bill Reminders**.
2. Tap **+ Add Real Bill**.
3. Fill in: name, category, amount, and the next real due date.
4. Tick **"This bill repeats"** if it's a recurring bill (most are) and pick how often —
   Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, Every 3 Months, or Yearly. Leave it unticked for a
   one-off bill (like a one-time fee) — it'll mark itself complete after you pay it.
5. Choose how many days before the due date you want to be reminded (default: 2 days).
6. Save. That's it — you'll get exactly one reminder per billing cycle, timed to your
   lead setting.

**When you've actually paid it in real life**, tap **Paid**. If it's recurring, the due
date automatically rolls forward to the next cycle and the reminder resets — you never
have to re-create the same bill every month. If it was a one-off, it's marked done.

> **Why only one reminder, not a daily nag?** Because notification fatigue is real, and
> a tool you start muting is a tool that stopped working. One well-timed reminder beats
> five ignored ones.

### 🎯 Real Savings Goals

Track savings goals for things you're *actually* saving toward — a phone, a trip, an
emergency fund, school fees — with a level of detail the in-game Lengo tool doesn't need,
because this one is permanent and tied to your account (not just this browser).

**How it works:**
1. Open **Real Savings Goals** → **+ New Goal**.
2. Give it a name, a target amount, and (optionally) a target date.
3. You can hold **as many goals as you like**, all at once.
4. Tap any goal to open it and see its own **live progress ring**, exactly how much
   you've saved of the target, and its full **deposit history** — every contribution you
   ever logged, with the date and an optional note ("From side hustle," "Birthday money"
   — whatever helps you remember).
5. Every time you actually put money aside in real life, log it as a deposit — amount,
   date, note. The ring and percentage update instantly.
6. **The moment your total deposits reach the target, the goal marks itself Completed
   automatically** — no button to press, just a small celebration and a permanent record
   that you did it. It moves visibly into your completed goals so you can look back on
   it whenever you want.

## 4. Notifications — how they work, and how to control them

Real Bill Reminders arrive as push notifications through the **same trusted system**
every other PesaQuest alert uses:

- **Quiet hours (9:30pm–6am)** — you will never be woken up by a bill reminder.
- **A daily cap** — so even on a busy day, you're not buried in notifications.
- **A dedicated category** — go to **Profile → Notification Settings** and you'll find
  **"🌍 Real-life bill reminders"** as its own toggle, separate from in-game alerts. Turn
  it off any time without affecting anything else.

---

## 5. Why keep the game and real life so clearly separate?

Because mixing them would undermine both. If a real bill reminder ever nudged your Pesa
City balance, you'd stop trusting the game's numbers. If real savings deposits ever
gave you in-game XP, you'd stop trusting that the game rewards genuine effort rather
than real-world wealth you may or may not already have. Keeping a hard, visible wall
between "practice" and "real" is what makes it safe to practice boldly in the game *and*
trust the tools that follow you into real life.

**The whole arc, start to finish:** learn the concept in a quest → rehearse the maths in
a calculator → apply it for real with a Real Bill Reminder or a Real Savings Goal. That's
the entire design philosophy of PesaQuest in one sentence — and the Smart Money Tools are
where step two quietly becomes step three.
